# PART 8 — YOUR RESULTS

### Q56. What is your accuracy?

Say exactly:

> **"Our best validation accuracy after fine-tuning was approximately 97.42% on the PlantVillage validation set."**

Don't simply say:

> "Our model is 97.42% accurate."

---

### Q57. What is accuracy?

> "Accuracy is the proportion of predictions that the model classified correctly out of all predictions."

Formula:

\[
Accuracy = \frac{Correct\ Predictions}{Total\ Predictions}
\]

---

### Q58. What is precision?

> "Precision tells us, out of the images the model predicted as a particular class, how many were actually that class."

Formula:

\[
Precision = \frac{TP}{TP+FP}
\]

---

### Q59. What is recall?

> "Recall tells us, out of all the actual images belonging to a class, how many the model successfully detected."

Formula:

\[
Recall = \frac{TP}{TP+FN}
\]

---

### Q60. What is F1-score?

> "F1-score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall. It gives us a balance between false positives and false negatives."

\[
F1=2\times\frac{Precision\times Recall}{Precision+Recall}
\]

---

### Q61. What is your F1-score?

Your PPT reports:

> **Weighted F1 = 0.9743**

and:

> **Macro F1 = 0.9668**

So say:

> "Our weighted F1-score is 0.9743, or approximately 97.43%, and our macro F1-score is 0.9668."

---

### Q62. Difference between macro F1 and weighted F1?

This is a very likely question.

> **"Macro F1 calculates the F1-score for every class and gives every class equal importance. Weighted F1 also calculates class-wise F1, but gives more weight to classes that contain more samples."**

Simple:

```text
Macro F1
→ every class equally important

Weighted F1
→ larger classes have more influence
```

---

### Q63. Why can accuracy and F1-score be different?

> "Accuracy looks at the overall percentage of correct predictions, while F1 considers precision and recall for individual classes. Therefore, F1 can reveal class-specific weaknesses that overall accuracy may hide."

---

### Q64. What is validation loss?

> "It measures how different the model's predicted probability distribution is from the correct labels on the validation data. Lower loss generally indicates better performance."

---

### Q65. Your validation loss was?

> **Approximately 0.0754 at the best fine-tuning result.**
